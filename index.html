WHAT IT IS

      
ESCAPE 99 is a light dungeon escape game. Every room asks one question — “I understand what I need to do… so how do I get there?” — then hands you a key, a trap, and a door. Rooms take 20–60 seconds. Death restarts instantly.

      

        
Find the key
The exit stays locked until the golden key is in your hand.

        
Read the danger
Spikes warn before they strike. Fire walls hunt you. Blue = safe, red = death, gold = reward.

        
Collect & spend
Coins buy simple upgrades: an extra heart, a stronger blade, a dash, a treasure magnet.

        
Three stars
Escape · take every coin · clear the room challenge. Never forced, always worth it.

FEATURES IN THIS BUILD

      

        
Original art
Arin and every prop are drawn procedurally at runtime — 15 animations, zero image assets, instant load.

        
Synthesised audio
Music and SFX are generated with WebAudio and rise in intensity when the fire starts.

        
How it's built
Vanilla ES modules, no framework, no build step, no dependencies. Data-driven rooms.

        
Accessible
Reduced screen shake, high-contrast hazards, vibration off, joystick or swipe, left-handed layout, English + हिन्दी.
